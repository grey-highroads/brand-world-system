# Production Modes and Creative Control Policy

> Status: Placeholder. The initial policy will be drafted after the brand-world schema.

## Purpose

Define how the system decides when to invent and when to obey while producing work from the same brand brain.

## Initial scope

- Constrained, editorial, and hybrid modes
- Locked, flexible, and prohibited elements
- Inference permissions and asset-drift tolerances

- Evaluation priorities and approval thresholds
- Revision behavior and protection from unrequested changes
- Failure conditions and escalation paths
- Output packaging and production records

The completed policy will show how one request is compiled and evaluated differently under each production mode.
