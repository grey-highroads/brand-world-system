# Focused Needs Review validation checklist

Use this against a real client brain before merging the interaction into the permanent Brand Brain renderer.

- Enter Needs Review with multiple unresolved rulings.
- Confirm the global sidebar collapses and `Exit review` returns to Brand Brain overview.
- Confirm passive Protections status disappears when nothing is pending.
- Confirm proposed protections still appear when a ruling is required.
- Confirm one ruling appears at a time.
- Confirm evidence starts closed and opens from `Why am I seeing this?`.
- Choose the first answer and confirm the existing decision is persisted.
- Confirm the next unresolved ruling becomes active after the save render.
- Use Previous and Next to revisit rulings without changing them.
- Change an earlier answer and confirm the new choice persists.
- Open `View all decisions`, jump to another ruling, and confirm the drawer closes.
- Resolve the final ruling and confirm the completion state replaces the decision surface.
- From completion, choose `Review decisions`, open a resolved ruling, and confirm it stays editable.
- Choose `Done reviewing` and confirm the completion state returns.
- Use the existing final action and confirm Brand Brain draft/candidate transition behavior is unchanged.
- Repeat with an incremental update where the active brain version must stay in production until candidate approval.
- Repeat at a narrow viewport and confirm no horizontal overflow or clipped decision controls.
